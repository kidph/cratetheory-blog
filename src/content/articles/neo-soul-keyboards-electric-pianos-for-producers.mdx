Keyboards and electric pianos play a vital role in shaping the soundscapes of neo soul music.

### FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between a classic keyboard and an analog synth?**

A: Classic keyboards like the Fender Rhodes or B3 organ are designed to mimic acoustic instruments, while analog synths like the Sequential Prophet-6 or Arturia MiniFreak produce unique electronic tones.

**Q: Can I use these keyboards and electric pianos in a digital audio workstation (DAW)?**

A: Yes, most of the products mentioned can be used as VST plugins within your DAW, allowing you to seamlessly integrate them into your productions.

**Q: What's the best way to process my keyboard and electric piano sounds?**

A: Experiment with various effects like reverb, delay, and compression to create depth and interest in your sound. Don't be afraid to push the limits of what's possible – it's all about finding that perfect balance between warmth and clarity.
