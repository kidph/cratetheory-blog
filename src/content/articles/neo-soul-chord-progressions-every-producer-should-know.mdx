# 10 Essential Neo Soul Chord Progressions for Producers to Elevate Their Sound
===========================================================

As a producer of neo soul music, you know the importance of crafting chord progressions that evoke the emotional depth and intimacy of classic soul records. In this article, we'll explore 10 essential neo soul chord progressions to help elevate your sound.

## The Heartbeat of Neo Soul: Essential Chord Progressions
--------------------------------------------------------

Neo soul is all about blending traditional R&B with modern production techniques to create a unique sonic identity. A key element in achieving this blend is the use of chord progressions that pay homage to classic soul while incorporating contemporary twists. Here are 10 essential neo soul chord progressions to get you started:

### 1. The Classic Minor: i - vi - ii - V
--------------------------------------------

This progression, popularized by artists like D'Angelo and Erykah Badu, is a staple of the neo soul genre. To create this progression, use minor chords (e.g., Am7) in place of major chords to add tension and depth.

### 2. The Extended Chord: I - bVII - i
-----------------------------------------

This progression is perfect for creating a sense of drama and resolution. Use extended chords (e.g., 9th or 11th chords) to add complexity and interest.

### 3. The Modal Interchange: i - VII - ii - V
------------------------------------------------

Modal interchange involves borrowing chords from parallel modes or scales to create new, interesting sounds. This progression is a great example of modal interchange in action.

### 4. The Minor-Major Tension: i - vii° - V
---------------------------------------------

This progression creates a sense of tension between the minor and major keys, adding emotional depth to your music.

### 5. The Neo Soul Staple: I - IV - ii - V
------------------------------------------

This progression is a variation on the classic I-IV-V progression, but with a modern twist. Use minor chords in place of major chords to create a more contemporary sound.

### 6. The Suspended Chord: i - iii° - vii° - V
---------------------------------------------------

Suspended chords add a sense of uncertainty and tension to your music. This progression is perfect for creating a moody, atmospheric sound.

### 7. The Altered Dominant: i - V7alt - ii - V
--------------------------------------------------

This progression incorporates altered dominants (e.g., flat 5th or sharp 9th) to create a sense of tension and release.

### 8. The Neapolitan Chord: II - V - I
-----------------------------------------

The Neapolitan chord is a minor chord built on the second scale degree. This progression creates a sense of surprise and drama.

### 9. The Pedal Point: i - V - i
----------------------------------

A pedal point involves repeating a note or chord over changing harmonies to create a sense of stability and continuity.

### 10. The Chromatic Passing Chord: i - bVII - ii - V
--------------------------------------------------------

This progression incorporates chromatic passing chords (e.g., flat 5th or sharp 9th) to create a sense of tension and release.
